# ChargeVerse Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

ChargeVerse is a charging companion app that shows animated artwork and live
charging information. This policy explains what the app does with data.

## Data we collect

**None.** ChargeVerse does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any
personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics
or tracking.

## Data used on your device

- **Battery information** (level, charging state, current, voltage,
  temperature, estimated time to full) is read from your device to show the
  charging screen. It is processed only on your device and never leaves it.
- **Your preferences** (selected theme, whether "Open when charging" is on)
  are saved only on your device.

## Network use

The app connects to the internet only to download the scene videos, from
GitHub (github.com and its file servers), the first time you choose a video
theme. These requests contain no personal information. GitHub may log
standard technical details such as your IP address, under
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
Downloaded videos are stored in the app's private storage.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet | Download scene videos |
| Notifications | Show the "ChargeVerse is ready" notification and open the charging screen when you plug in |
| Foreground service | Watch for the charger while "Open when charging" is on |
| Full-screen notifications | Open the charging screen over the lock screen |
| Run at startup | Restart the charger watcher after the phone reboots |

"Open when charging" is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at
any time with the ⚙ button in the app.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
